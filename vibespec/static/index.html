Turn your guests' taste into a hotel experience you can defend.

Cultural data can tell you that people who love Muji and Patagonia also love a certain band, book or restaurant. It cannot tell you whether that band suits a calm lobby, or whether that restaurant is still open. VibeSpec discovers with Qloo, checks every find against your brief, revises when the evidence falls short, and tells you what still needs checking.

Build your own

The example runs on real Qloo data: an eco-minded 40-room hotel in downtown Manhattan, anchored on Muji and Patagonia. About 25 seconds.

How it works

  1. 1

    Discover with Qloo

    Your anchor brands are matched to Qloo entities, and Qloo returns artists, books, brands and places their audiences have affinity for, with each anchor's share.

  2. 2

    Check fit with your hotel

    Every candidate is tested against your brief using Qloo's own descriptions, genres, hours and categories. Missing evidence stays "unverified", never "fits".

  3. 3

    Revise and explain

    When too few candidates fit, a rule-based agent re-queries for more, then writes the proposal with every check you still need to make. No language model decides anything.

What Qloo provides

Cultural affinity between brands, artists, books and places, with per-anchor explainability, plus descriptive metadata.

What VibeSpec never invents

Supplier prices, inventory, contracts or revenue. Budget figures are your own assumptions or clearly labelled as indicative.
